# 儿童双语《水浒传》第 1 期｜龙虎山奇遇：108 颗星星飞上天！
Water Margin for Kids Ep. 1 | Adventure on Dragon-Tiger Mountain: 108 Stars Fly into the Sky!


【故事导读】

小朋友，你看过夜晚满天闪烁的星星吗？  
Little friend, have you ever watched the twinkling stars fill the night sky?  

在很久很久以前的宋朝，一场神奇的“流星雨”，拉开了中国四大名著之一《水浒传》的序幕！  
Long, long ago in the Song Dynasty, a magical shower of shooting stars opened the curtain on Water Margin, one of China's greatest classic stories!  

今天，就让我们跟着一位好奇的大官，一起爬上云雾缭绕的龙虎山，看看那 108 颗神奇的星星是怎么飞向天空的吧！  
Today, let us follow a curious official up misty Dragon-Tiger Mountain and see how 108 magical stars flew into the sky!  


## 1. 爬上云雾缭绕的龙虎山

很久以前，京城里突然闹起了一场大瘟疫，许多百姓都生病了。  
Long ago, a terrible illness swept through the capital city, and many people fell sick.  

皇帝非常着急，派了一位名叫洪太尉的大官，骑着白马前往遥远的龙虎山，请一位德高望重的老道长下山帮忙祈福。  
The emperor was very worried, so he sent a high official named General Hong on a white horse to faraway Dragon-Tiger Mountain to ask a wise old master for help.  

龙虎山真美呀！到处是翠绿的松树、叮咚流淌的山泉，还有飘在半空中的白云。  
Dragon-Tiger Mountain was breathtaking! Everywhere there were emerald pine trees, singing mountain streams, and soft white clouds floating in the air.

## 2. 一座神秘的“禁地大殿”

办完正事后，洪太尉在山上到处逛风景。  
After finishing his mission, General Hong strolled around the mountain to enjoy the views.  

走着走着，他看到了一座非常古老的大殿。大门上挂着一把沉甸甸的大铜锁，门前安安静静，连一只小鸟都不敢靠近。  
As he walked, he spotted a very ancient hall. A heavy brass lock hung on the grand doors, and it was so quiet that not even a little bird dared come near.  

洪太尉好奇极了，问身旁的老道长：“这里面藏着什么宝贝呀？快打开让我瞧瞧！”  
General Hong was bursting with curiosity and asked the old master beside him, "What treasure is hidden inside? Open it up and let me see!"  

老道长赶紧摆摆手说：“千万不行！这座大殿里封印着一百零八颗神奇的星宿，祖师爷叮嘱过，绝对不能打开！”  
The old master quickly waved his hands and said, "Please don't! Inside this hall are 108 magical stars sealed away long ago. We must never open it!"

## 3. 轰隆一声，108 颗金星冲上云霄！

可是，好奇心太重的洪太尉根本不听劝，坚持让人打开了铜锁，走进了大殿。  
However, General Hong was far too curious to listen. He insisted on unlocking the doors and stepped inside.  

大殿里空荡荡的，只在正中央立着一块刻满花纹的石碑。洪太尉又让人把石碑下的大石板用力撬开。  
The hall was empty except for a carved stone tablet in the center. General Hong ordered his men to lift the heavy stone slab underneath.  

突然，“轰隆”一声巨响！一道耀眼的金光从地底喷涌而出，化作一百零八颗闪闪发亮的金色星星，像一百零八条欢快的小流星，一下子冲破屋顶，飞向了广阔的天空！  
Suddenly, BOOM! A dazzling beam of golden light burst from the ground and turned into 108 sparkling golden stars. Like joyful shooting stars, they zoomed right through the roof into the wide sky!  

洪太尉吓得目瞪口呆，老道长却望着天空长叹一声：“哎呀，这 108 颗星星飞向人间，今后江湖上可要热闹起来喽！”  
General Hong stared in shock, while the old master gazed at the sky and sighed, "Oh my! Those 108 stars have flown into the world—great adventures are about to begin!"

## 4. 一百零八位好汉的故事开始了

那一晚，108 颗璀璨的星星划过夜空，落向了大宋朝的四面八方。  
That night, the 108 glowing stars streaked across the sky and landed in every corner of the Song Dynasty.  

有的落进了热闹的集市，有的落进了幽静的山林，还有的落进了碧波荡漾的水泊梁山。  
Some fell into busy markets, some into quiet forests, and some near the sparkling waters of Liangshan Marsh.  

原来，他们就是后来行侠仗义、聪明勇敢的 108 位梁山好汉！从下一期开始，我们就一起去认识第一位力大无穷的大英雄——花和尚鲁智深吧！  
They were destined to become the 108 brave, clever, and kind-hearted heroes of Liangshan! In our next episode, let us meet our very first mighty hero—Lu Zhishen!

## 📌 本期难点词汇小词典 / Key Words to Know

1. curious（好奇的）：形容特别想知道秘密的心情。  
例句：General Hong was very curious.（洪太尉非常好奇。）  

2. ancient（古老的）：指存在了很久很久的东西。  
例句：He spotted a very ancient hall.（他看到了一座非常古老的大殿。）  

3. sealed（封印的、封住的）：紧紧关起来、不让打开。  
例句：108 stars were sealed inside.（108 颗星星被封印在里面。）  

4. dazzling（耀眼的、闪闪发光的）：亮得让人睁不开眼。  
例句：A dazzling golden light burst out.（一道耀眼的金光喷涌而出。）  

5. adventure（冒险、奇遇）：充满惊喜和挑战的旅程。  
例句：Great adventures are about to begin!（精彩的冒险就要开始啦！）
