# 儿童双语《水浒传》第 3 期｜力大无穷的花和尚：鲁智深倒拔垂杨柳！
Water Margin for Kids Ep. 3 | The Mighty Flowery Monk: Lu Zhishen Uproots a Giant Willow Tree!


【故事导读】

小朋友，你见过力气大到能把一棵大树连根拔起的人吗？  
Little friend, have you ever met someone strong enough to pull a giant tree right out of the ground?  

上一期见义勇为的鲁提辖，为了躲避官府追查，剃光了头发，穿上了袈裟，变成了一位豪爽又可爱的和尚，法号“鲁智深”！  
To stay safe after helping Little Lotus, brave Captain Lu shaved his head, put on a monk's robe, and became a cheerful, big-hearted monk named Lu Zhishen!  

今天，他来到了京城大相国寺看管菜园，快来看看他是怎么用惊人的力气，把一群调皮鬼治得服服帖帖的吧！  
Today, he arrives to look after the vegetable garden at Great Xiangguo Temple—let us see how his incredible strength wins over a group of mischievous troublemakers!  


## 1. 新来的菜园管理员

这一天，寺庙里的方丈派鲁智深去照看一片大大的菜园。  
One bright morning, the temple's head monk asked Lu Zhishen to take care of a large vegetable garden.  

菜园里种满了绿油油的白菜、萝卜，墙边还长着几棵又高又粗的垂杨柳。  
The garden was filled with fresh green cabbages and radishes, with tall, thick weeping willow trees growing beside the wall.  

鲁智深扛着他的大禅杖，笑眯眯地走进菜园，觉得这里又安静又舒服，真是个好地方！  
Carrying his heavy iron staff over his shoulder, Lu Zhishen walked in with a big smile, thinking how peaceful and lovely the garden was!

## 2. 一群爱搞恶作剧的调皮鬼

可是，菜园附近住着二三十个爱捣蛋的泼皮。他们平时总喜欢欺负新来的和尚，偷摘菜园里的瓜果。  
However, twenty or thirty mischievous troublemakers lived nearby. They loved playing pranks on new monks and sneaking fruit from the garden.  

听说新来了一位胖大和尚，几个调皮鬼躲在墙角偷偷商量：“咱们去给他一个下马威，把他吓跑吧！”  
Hearing that a new monk had arrived, they whispered behind the garden wall, "Let's play a trick on him and scare him away!"  

没想到，鲁智深早就一眼看穿了他们的小心思，既不生气也不害怕，反而哈哈大笑，三两下就把最爱捣蛋的两个人稳稳按在地上，吓得大家赶紧连连求饶！  
Little did they know, Lu Zhishen saw right through their plan! Instead of getting angry, he laughed heartily and effortlessly pinned the two ringleaders down until everyone begged for mercy!

## 3. 轰隆！徒手拔起大柳树

第二天，这群调皮鬼彻底服气了，还专门带着点心来向鲁智深赔礼道歉。大家正坐在树下开心聊天，突然头顶的垂杨柳上有几只乌鸦“哇哇”乱叫，吵得大家听不清说话。  
The next day, the troublemakers apologized with tasty snacks. While everyone chatted happily under a tall willow tree, noisy crows perched overhead began cawing loudly.  

调皮鬼们说：“师父，我们搬梯子去把鸟窝挪走吧！”  
The troublemakers said, "Master, let's fetch a ladder to move the nest!"  

鲁智深摆摆手，笑着说：“不用梯子那么麻烦，看我的！”  
Lu Zhishen waved his hand and chuckled, "No need for a ladder—watch this!"  

只见他脱下外袍，扎稳马步，右手朝下、左手朝上，死死抱住那棵水桶粗的大垂杨柳，大喝一声“起！”——“咔嚓”一声，整棵大柳树竟然被他连根拔了起来！  
Rolling up his robe, he planted his feet firmly, wrapped his mighty arms around the thick willow trunk, and shouted, "UP!" With a mighty crack, he lifted the entire willow tree right out of the earth!

## 4. 调皮鬼变成了好朋友

围观的调皮鬼们全都看呆了，一个个竖起大拇指喊道：“师父真是天神下凡呀！我们以后再也不捣蛋了，全都听您的！”  
The amazed troublemakers stared with wide eyes and cheered, "Master, you are truly incredible! We'll never cause trouble again!"  

鲁智深哈哈大笑，不仅没有摆架子，还热心地教大家强身健体、练习武艺。从那以后，菜园里再也没有恶作剧，每天都充满了欢声笑语！  
Lu Zhishen laughed warmly and gladly taught them how to stay strong and practice martial arts. From that day on, the garden was filled with laughter and friendship!  

下一期，我们将认识威风凛凛的枪法大师——“豹子头”林冲，去看看他在风雪之夜的勇敢故事！  
In our next episode, we will meet the legendary spear master—Panther Head Lin Chong—and follow his brave adventure on a snowy night!

## 📌 本期难点词汇小词典 / Key Words to Know

1. mischievous（调皮的、爱捣蛋的）：喜欢搞小恶作剧的样子。  
例句：Mischievous troublemakers lived nearby.（附近住着爱捣蛋的调皮鬼。）  

2. prank（恶作剧、捉弄）：故意开的小玩笑或捣蛋。  
例句：They loved playing pranks.（他们喜欢搞恶作剧。）  

3. uproot（连根拔起）：把整棵树或植物从泥土里整个拔出来。  
例句：Lu Zhishen uproots the giant willow tree!（鲁智深把大柳树连根拔起！）  

4. incredible（难以置信的、了不起的）：厉害得让人不敢相信。  
例句：Master, you are truly incredible!（师父，您真是太了不起了！）  

5. friendship（友谊、友情）：好朋友之间温暖的情谊。  
例句：The garden was filled with friendship.（菜园里充满了友谊。）
